# Watching, comments and notifications

## Comments

Comments sit beneath a page and thread one level deep. You need permission to
comment, which is separate from permission to edit — a page can be open to
discussion without being open to changes.

## Watching

The bell on a page or space starts watching it. You are then notified about
changes to it.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar carries a count and opens the list. You are notified
when:

- a page you watch changes;
- someone replies to your comment;
- someone mentions you;
- a sync bundle lands that affects something you watch.

Notifications only ever reference things you can still read. If a page becomes
invisible to you after a notification was created, the notification goes with
it rather than becoming a hint that something exists.
